<objective>

them adopted at a human-verify checkpoint (D-08), and wire the approved numbers into the framework
source of truth. Two-tier per metric (D-07): a "must-hold" floor derived from the measured baseline (no
Phase 6+ candidate may regress it) and an aspirational target derived from business need (~500x20

candidate evaluation. This is the gate that turns the baseline into the ruler Phase 6 uses.

</objective>
